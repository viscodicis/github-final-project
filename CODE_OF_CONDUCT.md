
# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:
- Showing respect and empathy toward others.
- Respecting different opinions and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, publishing private information without permission, and other inappropriate conduct.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for explaining and enforcing these standards. They may remove or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct and communicate the reasons for their decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and situations in which someone officially represents the project.

## Enforcement

Unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the project maintainers through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed fairly and promptly, while respecting the privacy of those involved.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers may apply the following measures according to the severity and impact of a violation:

1. **Correction:** A private warning explaining the inappropriate behavior.
2. **Warning:** A formal warning with restrictions on interactions.
3. **Temporary Ban:** Temporary suspension from project participation.
4. **Permanent Ban:** Permanent removal from project participation for serious or repeated violations.
